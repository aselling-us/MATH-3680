Below is the series expansion for $e^x$.
$$e^x = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{x^n}{n!}$$

This is the first 10 digits of $\pi$ which is approximately $\frac{22}{7}$.
$$\pi \approx 3.1415926535$$

This is the quadratic formula which gives the solutions to the equation $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Slope intercept form is also useful when trying to plot lines only given the slope, $m$, and a point $(x,y)$
$$y=mx+b$$

And, lastly, I wanted to include the famous Pythagorean Theorem, which is
$$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$
and allows you to compute $c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$, where $c$ is the hypotenuse from legs $a$ and $b$.
